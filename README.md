# CirculaTech

> **AI-Powered Demolition LiDAR & Reclaimed Material Marketplace**  
> *Turning demolition sites into certified reclaimed material inventories.*

## 🏗️ Overview

The construction and demolition (C&D) industry produces over one-third of all waste generated globally (over 600M tons in the US alone). Most discarded timber, structural steel, architectural stone, and reusable fixtures end up in landfills due to the prohibitive cost and time required to manually inspect, catalog, and certify reclaimed materials for resale. Meanwhile, developers face strict carbon regulations and green building quotas (LEED, BREEAM).

**CirculaTech** is an on-site mobile scanning and computer vision platform that turns demolition sites into reclaimed materials stores before the wrecking ball swings.

---

## ⚡ Core Architecture

1. **Spatial Scanning & Multimodal 3D Vision:**
   - Contractors walk through pre-demolition buildings with mobile LiDAR and video.
   - Multimodal 3D vision models identify, dimension, grade, and catalog reusable elements (structural steel beams, hardwoods, copper, HVAC units, brick).

2. **Automated Structural Compliance Check:**
   - Machine learning algorithms match reclaimed elements against AISC 360-16, ASTM A6/A992, and municipal building code tolerances.
   - Generates digital material passports and PE stamp-ready engineering dossiers.

3. **Automated B2B Circular Marketplace:**
   - Automatically lists certified reclaimed materials to architects and general contractors seeking low-embodied-carbon supplies to fulfill LEED v4.1 and ESG mandates.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Interactive 3-Stage Scanning Walkthrough:** Visual explanatory pipeline from spatial LiDAR sweep, to structural code compliance check, to circular marketplace listing.
- **Embodied Carbon & Reclaimed Salvage Value Calculator:** Real-time dynamic estimator for diverted tonnage, avoided CO₂e, avoided landfill tipping fees, and projected resale revenue.
- **Live Reclaimed Inventory Feed:** Certified catalog with instant inspection dossiers and escrow reserves.
- **Transparent 3-Tier Pricing Model:** Field Scanner, Commercial Pro, and Enterprise Circular packages.
- **Light & Dark Mode:** Industrial tech UI with laser-cyan and electric-emerald accents.
